# CAEDM News Desk: user guide

For the Communications team of the Catholic Archdiocese of Edmonton. This guide is also shown in the app under
**User guide**.

## What News Desk is

News Desk is the Communications team's own news monitor. It watches a short list of approved sources: the
Archdiocese, Catholic schools and partners, Canadian Catholic and Vatican news, and the Government of Alberta. It
gathers their new stories in one place.

It then:

- ranks every story from **P0 Critical** to **P3 Background**, so the important ones stand out;
- emails the team a **morning brief**, and each person their own **personal brief** at the time they choose;
- sends an **alert** straight away when something urgent appears;
- tracks **follow-ups**, so every story that needs action has an owner and a due date.

News Desk never writes its own summaries and never uses AI. Each story shows the publisher's own headline and short
description, with a link to the original page. Always check the original before you share or act on a story.

## A day with News Desk

1. **Through the night and morning**, News Desk checks every approved source for new stories. Each new story is
   given a priority straight away.
2. **If something urgent appears** (P0, or P1 during alert hours), an alert email goes to the team mailbox, and to
   anyone who turned alerts on for themselves.
3. **At 8:00 am** (the time is set in Settings), the team brief goes to the Communications mailbox.
4. **At each person's chosen time**, their personal brief arrives.
5. **During the day**, staff review stories, assign follow-ups and mark them covered. While anyone is signed in, News
   Desk keeps checking sources, and a banner appears when new stories arrive.

All times are Edmonton time, and daylight saving changes are handled automatically.

## What the priorities mean

| Priority | Meaning | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **P0 Critical** | Serious, time-sensitive news directly involving the Archdiocese or a local Catholic institution, or a papal transition | Alert sent immediately, at any hour |
| **P1 High** | Important local Catholic, education, healthcare or policy news that needs timely review | Alert sent during alert hours (7:00 am to 9:00 pm by default) |
| **P2 Standard** | Relevant community news, events and routine announcements | Included in the briefs |
| **P3 Background** | Context, including routine international news | Included in the briefs |

Priorities come from keyword rules that administrators can adjust in **Settings**. Every story page explains why it
received its priority. Managers and administrators can change a story's priority by hand, and the change is
recorded.

Stories that mention sensitive or unverified claims are labelled **Needs human review**. They report what a
publisher said, not established facts.

## Your personal news brief

Your personal brief is a summary of new stories, emailed to you at the time you choose.

**To turn it on:** go to **My account → Email notifications → My news brief** and set:

- **Time:** any half hour from 5:00 am to 9:00 pm, or "Same as the team brief".
- **Days:** every day, or weekdays only (Monday to Friday).
- **Include:** *Catholic news only* (the Archdiocese, Catholic partners, Canadian Catholic and Vatican sources), or
  *All monitored news* (adds government and other sources).

Then click **Save my settings**.

How it behaves:

- Each brief covers the news since your previous one, at least the last 24 hours, so nothing is missed or repeated.
  Monday's weekday brief covers the weekend.
- Stories are grouped into sections: top stories, Edmonton Catholic news, partner updates, Alberta education,
  healthcare and policy, Canadian Catholic and Vatican news, and upcoming events.
- The follow-ups assigned to you are listed at the end.
- If nothing new was published, no email is sent that day. The outbox shows it as *skipped*.
- It can arrive up to 15 minutes after the time you chose.
- Every brief has a link at the bottom to change its time or content, or turn it off.

**Want it right now?** In the **Email outbox**, click **Send me a brief now**. You'll get the last 24 hours
immediately, with your usual Catholic-only or All-news choice. It doesn't affect your scheduled brief.

## Team brief and alerts

The **team brief** goes to the Communications mailbox (communications@caedm.ca) every morning. Unlike personal
briefs, it never repeats a story an earlier team brief already reported. Administrators set its time and mailbox in
**Settings**.

**Alerts** are short emails about P0 and P1 stories, sent as they happen. To avoid flooding inboxes, there are at
most 3 alerts an hour and 10 a day by default. Choose what you personally receive in **My account**: all alerts,
P0 only, or none.

## The pages

### Overview

Today at a glance:

- new stories in the last 24 hours;
- high-priority stories still open;
- stories that need review;
- the health of each source;
- your open follow-ups.

Administrators also see pending access requests here.

### News Feed

Every story, newest first. You can search, and filter by source, priority, status and date.

The workflow for each story is **New → Reviewing → Assigned → Covered** (or **Archived** when no action is needed).

Use **Submit a link** to add a story from a source News Desk doesn't monitor. It is marked *unverified* until
someone checks it.

### Story page

The full record of one story:

- the publisher's description and the link to the original;
- why it got its priority;
- its history;
- notes from the team.

From here you can change its status, assign a follow-up with a due date, or add a note.

### Follow-ups

Everything assigned and not yet done, with overdue items flagged. Overdue follow-ups also appear in the team brief.
When you are assigned a follow-up, you get an email, unless you turned that off in My account.

### Sources

The approved sources, when each was last checked, and whether the check worked. A failing source is retried less
often until it recovers; the others keep working. Administrators can add sources here (see below).

### Email outbox

Every email News Desk has produced: team briefs, personal briefs, alerts, invitations, requests and security
notices. Open any message to see exactly what was sent.

Delivery statuses:

- **Sent**: delivered to the mail server.
- **Simulated**: live email is off, so it was stored here only and nothing was sent.
- **Failed**: the mail server couldn't be reached. It retries automatically after 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes.
  Managers can also click **Retry now**.
- **Skipped**: deliberately not sent, for example a personal brief on a day with nothing new.

Also on this page:

- **Send me a brief now** emails you a brief immediately.
- **Preview today's brief** shows the next team brief without sending it.
- **Alert dry run** shows what the next alert check would do.

### My account

Your name, your email notifications (personal brief, alerts, follow-up assignments, and for administrators, access
requests), and your password. Changing your password signs you out on your other devices and sends you a security
email.

### Settings (administrators)

Staff access and roles, the team brief time and mailbox, alert hours and limits, and the keyword rules behind the
priorities.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Read news, follow-ups and sources; manage their own account |
| **Member** | Everything a viewer can, plus review stories, manage follow-ups and notes, submit links |
| **Manager** | Everything a member can, plus change priorities, run brief and alert tools, check sources on demand |

Everyone can set up their own personal brief and use **Send me a brief now**.

## How News Desk keeps information trustworthy

- **Approved sources only.** A source is switched on only after its official feed or newsroom page and its
  robots.txt rules have been checked and recorded.
- **Publishers' words, not ours.** News Desk stores headlines, short publisher descriptions and links. It never
  copies full articles and never generates summaries.
- **Rules you can see.** Priorities come from published keyword rules, not AI. Each story shows why it was ranked as
  it was.
- **Clear labels.** Sensitive or unverified items are labelled, and submitted links stay *unverified* until checked.
- **Invitation-only access.** Only @caedm.ca addresses can be invited. After 5 wrong passwords, an account locks for
  15 minutes. Every change is recorded in an audit log.

## For administrators

**Adding staff.** In **Settings → Staff access**, enter a colleague's @caedm.ca email, choose their role and click
**Send invitation**. They
receive a one-time link, valid for 72 hours, to set their password. People can also request access from the
sign-in page; you'll see their request on the Overview.

**Adding a source.** In **Sources → Add source**:

1. Enter the organization's website.
2. Click **Find feed**, then **Test**.
3. Only sources with a working RSS or Atom feed, whose robots.txt allows monitoring, can be switched on.
4. Record the evidence in the source's notes.

**How the refresh works.** News Desk has no server running all the time. A refresh runs:

- every morning before the team brief;
- every few minutes while anyone is signed in;
- every 15 minutes from the scheduler.

Each refresh checks the sources that are due, sends alerts and any briefs that are due, and retries failed emails.
The refreshes take turns, so two never run at once, and each email is sent only once. If no refresh runs within 4
hours of a brief's time, that brief is skipped for the day rather than sent late.

## Questions

**I turned on my brief but nothing arrived.** Check three things:

- In the Email outbox, filter by **Personal briefs**. A *skipped* entry means nothing new was published that day.
- A *failed* entry means the mail server couldn't be reached. It will retry.
- Make sure the time you picked has already passed today, and that today matches your chosen days.

**Why is a story missing?** News Desk only sees the approved sources. Use **Submit a link** for anything else, or
ask an administrator to add the source.

**Why does a story have the wrong priority?** A manager can change it on the story page. If it keeps happening,
ask an administrator to adjust the keyword rules in Settings.

**Who do I contact?** Use **Help & support** in the sidebar.
